About Us

Our Story

My Choc. Shop is a small chocolate shop in Arau, Perlis. We started with a simple idea: to share good chocolate for every occasion, whether it is a gift, a celebration or a sweet treat for yourself.

We sell milk, dark and white chocolate, as well as popular brands and new flavours.


Why Choose Us

  • Wide variety of chocolates
  • Great offers and year end sales
  • Perfect for gifts
